# Core-literacy reference

Use the wording for the correct school stage. Select only one or two dimensions
as primary in one lesson.

## Dimensions by subject and stage

| Subject | Stage | Core-literacy dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese | compulsory education | cultural confidence, language use, thinking skills, aesthetic creativity |
| Chinese | senior high | language construction and use, development of thinking, aesthetic appreciation and creativity, cultural inheritance and understanding |
| English / Japanese / Russian | compulsory and senior high | language ability, cultural awareness, quality of thinking, learning ability |
| Mathematics | compulsory education | observe the real world mathematically, reason about it mathematically, and express it in mathematical language (the three mathematical capacities) |
| Mathematics | senior high | mathematical abstraction, logical reasoning, mathematical modeling, visual imagination, calculation, data analysis |
| Morality and law | compulsory education | political identification, moral development, understanding of the rule of law, healthy personal development, responsibility |
| Ideology and politics | senior high | political identification, scientific inquiry, awareness of the rule of law, public participation |
| History | compulsory and senior high | historical materialism, spatial and temporal understanding, evidence from historical sources, historical interpretation, commitment to community and country |
| Geography | compulsory and senior high | human-environment coordination, integrative thinking, regional understanding, geographical practice |
| Science | compulsory education | scientific concepts, scientific thinking, inquiry practice, attitudes and responsibility |
| Physics | compulsory and senior high | physical concepts, scientific thinking, scientific inquiry, scientific attitudes and responsibility |
| Chemistry | compulsory education | chemical concepts, scientific thinking, scientific inquiry and practice, scientific attitudes and responsibility |
| Chemistry | senior high | macroscopic identification and microscopic analysis, concepts of change and equilibrium, evidence-based reasoning and model understanding, scientific inquiry and innovation, scientific attitudes and social responsibility |
| Biology | compulsory education | concepts of life, scientific thinking, inquiry practice, attitudes and responsibility |
| Biology | senior high | concepts of life, scientific thinking, scientific inquiry, social responsibility |
| Information technology | compulsory education | information awareness, computational thinking, digital learning and innovation, responsibility in the information society |
| Arts | compulsory education | aesthetic perception, artistic expression, creative practice, cultural understanding |
| PE and health | compulsory and senior high | physical competence, healthy behavior, sporting ethics |
| Labor | compulsory education | understanding of work, practical work skills, work habits and qualities, work ethic |

For compulsory mathematics, use "three mathematical capacities" plus the stage's visible
manifestations:

- primary: number sense, quantity sense, symbol awareness, calculation skills, geometric intuition, spatial understanding, reasoning awareness,
  data awareness, model awareness, application awareness, innovation awareness;
- junior high: abstraction, calculation skills, geometric intuition, spatial understanding, reasoning, understanding of data,
  understanding of models, application awareness, innovation awareness.

## General design model

Core literacy forms while learners solve a problem in context. It is not a
knowledge label attached to a lecture.

- **Authentic context:** a concrete situation from learner life, society or a
  disciplinary phenomenon. It must generate a real unresolved problem.
- **Performance task:** an inspectable act such as explain, write, model,
  decide, design, test or present. It externalizes the selected dimension.
- **Learning activity:** the sequence learners need in order to complete the
  task: observe, operate, annotate, discuss, read, model, draft, test, compare
  and revise.
- **Performance assessment:** criteria aligned to the task, embedded during
  the work and at the end. The exit check is a transfer case, not a detached
  recall sheet.

## Technology, arts, PE and labor

These subjects default to practice, creation, operation, decision and public
display rather than content exposition:

- IT: identify an information problem, represent the process, build or modify
  a digital artifact, test it, then address responsible use.
- Arts: perceive a work or cultural situation, make an artistic choice,
  create or perform, explain the choice and revise from feedback.
- PE and health: perform, observe technique or behavior evidence, adjust, and
  connect the decision to health and sportsmanship.
- Labor: plan a real task, operate safely, inspect the product or service,
  improve it and reflect on habits, quality and responsibility.

Evaluate the real performance or artifact. A knowledge-recall quiz may support
diagnosis but cannot replace the task.
