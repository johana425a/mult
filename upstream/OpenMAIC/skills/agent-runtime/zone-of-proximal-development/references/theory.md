# Theoretical basis and design translation

This skill is instructional design inspired by zone-of-proximal-development theory. The sources below clarify concepts and support approaches; they do not establish that the OpenMAIC implementation improves learning or development.

## Zone of proximal development

Vygotsky, L. S. (1978). *Mind in Society*. Harvard University Press, pp. 85-91, especially p. 86.

[Scanned extract hosted by a third party](https://docdrop.org/static/drop-pdf/Vygotksy1978_ZPD-8W6rm.pdf). The source checked was an eight-page extract covering printed pages 84-91, rather than a publisher-provided complete book.

The text distinguishes independent problem solving from performance under adult guidance or collaboration with more capable peers, relating these to developing psychological functions and the relationship between learning and development. This skill therefore collects independent and assisted attempts while retaining task and support conditions. Interpreting one completed task as stable development exceeds what such a check supports.

## Scaffolding and proportionate assistance

Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976). The role of tutoring in problem solving. *Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17*, 89-100. [Publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x).

The account of tutoring/scaffolding concerns how a helper enables participation in tasks the learner cannot yet handle independently. Assistance changes with success, failure, and task demands. This supports waiting for responses before choosing help and preserving the learner's actions. The study's participants and tasks were limited; it does not establish a fixed number of hint levels for every subject.

## Modeling, coaching, and fading

Collins, A., Brown, J. S., & Holum, A. (1991). Cognitive apprenticeship: Making thinking visible. *American Educator*, Winter 1991. [Authors' article](https://www.aft.org/ae/winter1991/collins_brown_holum).

The article explains how modeling, scaffolding, fading, and coaching expose expert cognitive strategies and gradually shift responsibility to learners. Demonstrating another example, making key judgments visible, and reversible fading are design choices informed by it. Do not attribute all of these to a fixed teaching procedure devised by Vygotsky himself.

## What local verification establishes

Skill loading, responsive changes in model assistance, working pages, and response persistence are evidence about product behavior. Success on constructed test inputs is not evidence of real-student outcomes, stable development, or numerical ZPD measurement.
